## 🚀 Selected Work

### 🏛️ `federal-register-sentinel` 🔒 *(private — production client)*
Automated regulatory-intelligence pipeline replacing manual attorney review of the U.S. Federal Register (60,000+ pages/yr, 7 agencies). I owned ingestion and summarization; a teammate owned delivery. Deliberately biased the filter toward false negatives — missing a real regulation costs more than a wasted summary. **Live in production, in active use by a real legal team.**

`FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `GPT-4o-mini` `Railway`

---

### 🔍 [`aw-compliance-intelligence-db`](https://github.com/KshaunishHarsha/aw-compliance-intelligence-db)
Solo-built RAG platform over 1,617 compliance documents with citation-grounded chat — zero unsupported claims, enforced at the pipeline level, not just prompted for. Sequenced as a 7-phase build from ingestion to hardened deployment.

`Next.js` `pgvector` `Supabase` `GPT-4o`

---

### 🛡️ [`aegis-mumhack2025`](https://github.com/KshaunishHarsha/Aegis-MumHack2025)
6-agent forensics system detecting and attributing AI-generated video. Directed the tradeoff between accuracy and explainability — a weighted-fusion verdict over any single-signal approach. MumbaiHacks 2025 finalist.

`TensorFlow` `OpenCV` `Vertex AI`

---

### 🔬 [`frontier-fallback-safety-audit`](https://github.com/KshaunishHarsha/frontier-fallback-safety-audit)
225 evaluations across a 70B→8B model ladder. Found sycophancy flip rates scale-invariant at ~40–43% — a policy-relevant result on the risk of serving cheaper models under load. Presented at the Global South AI Safety Hackathon.

`Python` `OpenRouter` `Pandas`
